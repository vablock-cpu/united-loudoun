Our Core Ask

Four Actions the Board Must Take Now

The SCC ruling could issue any day. These four actions must happen before it does.

1

Take the Legal Steps Already on the Table

The Board has options right now that would clear the path for underground power lines — at zero cost to taxpayers. Accepting the open space easements and the additional parcel offered by the community removes viability barriers for underground routes.

2

Enforce the Standard the Board Set for Itself

The Board already said no high-voltage power lines within 500 feet of homes or schools. That resolution needs to be reaffirmed — clearly and on the record — so the SCC knows this community's position has not changed.

3

Tell the State and Dominion: No Overhead Lines Through Our Neighborhoods

The Board can file directly with the SCC. It has done it before. A clear statement that the Board will not support above-ground high-voltage power lines through residential communities carries real weight with the Commission.

4

Back the Bill That Puts These Lines Underground

HB 1487 / SB 827 has passed both chambers and specifically names Golden-to-Mars. A Board resolution supporting it is a statutory prerequisite for the underground pilot program. The cost: $0.99/month per household. The data centers driving this demand should foot the bill — not you.

Do NOT signal to the SCC that overhead through residential neighborhoods is acceptable
