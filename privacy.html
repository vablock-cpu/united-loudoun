Privacy Policy

Last revised: April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how United Loudoun (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with unitedloudoun.org (the “Site”).

What We Collect

Email address only. If you sign up for updates on the Site, we collect your email address. That is the only personal information we collect.

The letter-writing tool on this Site does not transmit any information to us. Names, addresses, and other details you type into the form are used only to build your letter locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

How We Use It

We use your email address solely to send updates about the Golden to Mars transmission line issue and related community advocacy efforts. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services

This Site uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy practices:

  • Formsubmit.co — processes email signups on our behalf.
  • Mapbox — powers the interactive route map. Mapbox may collect usage data when the map is loaded.
  • Google Fonts — serves web fonts. Google may log the IP address of requests for fonts.
  • Vercel — hosts the Site and may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited).

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain email addresses for as long as you remain subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us at contact@unitedloudoun.org and we will remove you promptly.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you by contacting us at contact@unitedloudoun.org.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
