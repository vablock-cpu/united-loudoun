Residents Urge Supervisors to Continue Push for Underground Power Lines

Loudoun Valley Estates residents appeared before county supervisors to oppose Dominion Energy's planned overhead transmission lines, citing EMF exposure near schools and declining property values. The SCC's three judges are expected to rule by month's end.

Residents Press Burying Transmission Line After Legislature Approves Pilot Program

With Virginia's General Assembly having approved an undergrounding pilot program, residents pressed their supervisors to keep demanding Dominion bury the Golden to Mars line. The SCC will have final say on the route.

Loudoun Times New March 19, 2026

The 2026 General Assembly Is Over. What Does It Mean for Loudoun?

Virginia's session wrapped with several bills relevant to Loudoun County, including the underground pilot program and data center tax exemptions. House and Senate remain at odds on details — governor action ahead.

Loudoun Times New March 15, 2026

Program to Bury Power Lines Passes State Legislature

Virginia's General Assembly passed a pilot program on March 14 allowing utilities to bury high-voltage lines where feasible, with localities covering half the added cost. Sen. Srinivasan and Del. Singh — both D-Loudoun — led the legislation specifically referencing Golden to Mars.
